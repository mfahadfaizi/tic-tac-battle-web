What Is Inside the Game

Designed around clean match flow, zero paywalls, and full offline/online parity.

01

Real-Time Online 1v1 Lobby

See active players currently online, challenge anyone in one tap via Firebase Cloud Messaging push notifications, and sync every move instantaneously over Firebase Realtime Database.

02

Minimax AI & Local Pass-and-Play

Play without internet against a Minimax game-tree opponent or sit side-by-side with a friend using custom player names and local match scoring.

03

ELO Ladder, XP & 12 Free Avatars

Every match updates your win/loss/draw totals, XP level bar, and ELO rating on the global leaderboard. All 12 player avatars are unlocked for everyone with no paid subscription.

04

Daily Credits & Match History

Get 10 free online play credits automatically each day, top up +3 anytime with an optional Rewarded Ad, and review full match logs stored in Cloud Firestore and local Hive storage.
