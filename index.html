Exciting Game Modes For Everyone

Whether you want a casual local round or high-stakes competitive online matches, Tic Tac Battle has you covered.

01

Real-Time Online Multiplayer (1v1)

Challenge friends or find live opponents worldwide. Select your preferred board dimension (3×3 up to 6×6) before launching the battle, exchange live reactions, and compete for global ranking.

02

Smart 3-Tier AI (Minimax Master)

Practice and sharpen your strategic thinking offline against Easy, Medium, or the Unbeatable Minimax AI that analyzes every possible branch of the game tree.

03

Local 2-Player Pass & Play

Perfect for road trips, flights, and gatherings! Play side-by-side on the same phone with friends and family using custom player names and complete offline scorekeeping.

04

Expanded 4x4, 5x5 & 6x6 Grids

Tired of predictable 3×3 ties? Test your tactical mind on larger grids: connect 4 on 4×4 and 5×5, or connect 5 on 6×6 for deep, Gomoku-inspired strategic gameplay.
