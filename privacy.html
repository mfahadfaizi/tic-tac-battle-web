Privacy Policy

How Tic Tac Battle collects, uses, and safeguards account details, game sessions, and advertising data.

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the mobile application Tic Tac Battle: Online & AI and its companion website. We believe in minimal data collection: you can play against the Minimax AI or in Local Pass & Play completely offline without creating an account or transmitting personal data.

2. Data Collected for Online Services

When you create an account to play online multiplayer, the following limited information is processed:

  • Account Credentials (Firebase Authentication): Your email address, display name, chosen avatar ID, and an encrypted User ID (UID).
  • Profile & Gameplay Statistics (Cloud Firestore): ELO rating, XP level, total wins/losses/draws, timestamped match history logs, virtual play ticket counters, and daily reward streak dates.
  • Live Online Match State (Firebase Realtime Database): Lobby presence, match challenge invitations, and live grid coordinates (3×3, 4×4, 5×5, 6×6).
  • Ephemeral In-Game Reactions: During an active online game, players may tap pre-defined emojis or short friendly phrases. These reactions are ephemeral, stored only in temporary memory during the active match session, never recorded in persistent databases or chat logs, and automatically purged when the game concludes.
  • Device Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging & Local Notifications): A device push token used solely to alert you of incoming match challenges and scheduled local reminders (such as hourly ticket refills and daily reward resets).

3. Advertising & Google AdMob

Tic Tac Battle is free to play and supported by Google AdMob. Depending on your region, AdMob and its certified ad network partners may collect device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), IP address, coarse location, and ad interaction telemetry to deliver non-personalized or personalized ads.

Ad-Free Entitlements: Players can earn temporary 100% ad-free gameplay by claiming the Day 7 weekly reward (1 hour ad-free) or completing the 5-Ad Challenge (30 minutes ad-free). VIP players (isPremium) have all interstitial navigation ads permanently disabled.

To opt out of personalized ads, visit your Android device settings under Settings → Google → Ads and enable “Delete advertising ID” or reset your ad settings.

4. Regional Rights: GDPR (UK & EU) & CCPA (United States)

We comply with international data protection standards, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the United Kingdom and European Union, and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) for US residents:

  • Right to Access: You have the right to request a copy of the personal data stored with your account.
  • Right to Rectification: You can update your display name and avatar at any time in your player profile.
  • Right to Erasure (Account Deletion): You can request complete deletion of your account, email, ELO ranking, and match history.
  • No Sale of Personal Data: We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal information to data brokers or third parties.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Your profile information is retained only as long as your account is active. To permanently delete your account and all associated records from Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication, submit an account removal request via our Support Form with your registered email address. Requests are processed within 48 hours.

6. Children’s Privacy

Tic Tac Battle is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.
