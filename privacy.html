Privacy Policy

How Tic Tac Battle handles account details, online match state, local offline storage, and Google AdMob advertising.

Effective: 2026

1. Data Collected by Feature

You can play offline against the Minimax AI or in Local Pass-and-Play without creating an account. If you register or sign in for Online Multiplayer, Tic Tac Battle processes the minimum data required:

  • Account Details (Firebase Authentication): Email address used for sign-in, display name, chosen avatar identifier, and your Firebase User ID (UID).
  • Player Profile & Stats (Cloud Firestore): ELO rating, XP, level, win/loss/draw counts, match history logs, and daily virtual play credit timestamps.
  • Live Match State (Firebase Realtime Database): Online lobby presence status, outgoing/incoming match challenges, and active 3×3 board moves.
  • Ephemeral In-Game Reactions: During active online matches, players may exchange pre-defined emojis and friendly canned phrases. These reactions are ephemeral, routed in volatile real-time memory during the active game session, are never stored in persistent databases or match history logs, and are automatically deleted when the game session ends.
  • Challenge & Engagement Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging & Local Notifications): An app-scoped device messaging token used to deliver multiplayer match invites, and scheduled local reminders (such as virtual Play Credit refills and daily match milestones).

2. Google AdMob Advertising

Tic Tac Battle is supported by Google AdMob using Banner, Interstitial, and optional Rewarded video ad units (which grant extra virtual Play Credits).

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process device advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID), IP address, coarse location signals, and ad interaction events to serve and measure ads in line with Google policies. Read more at Google Privacy & Terms. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads anytime in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

3. Local Storage on Your Device

  • Hive: Saves offline match history records locally on your device.
  • SharedPreferences: Tracks local virtual Play Credits balance, daily reset date, and ad cadence counters.

4. Publicly Visible Profile Fields

Your display name, avatar, ELO rating, and win/loss counts are visible to other signed-in players inside the Online Lobby and Leaderboard screens. Your authentication email address is never displayed publicly.

5. Data Sharing & Retention

We do not sell personal data. Infrastructure data is processed solely by Google Firebase and Google AdMob to run multiplayer gameplay and ad delivery. Local data is cleared when you uninstall the app or clear app storage.

6. Children's Privacy

Tic Tac Battle is not intended for children under 13 (or the applicable digital consent age in your country). If an account was created in error by a minor, contact us so it can be deleted.

7. Requesting Account & Data Deletion

To request permanent deletion of your Firebase Authentication account and Cloud Firestore profile data, choose Account / Data Deletion Request on our Contact Us page.

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent anytime via the Contact Us form.
