## Files

- `index.html` — Landing page with interactive 3x3 board preview, overview ledger, and app screenshots
- `privacy.html` — Privacy Policy covering Firebase services, Google AdMob, and local storage
- `terms.html` — Terms & Conditions and MIT license summary
- `contact.html` — HTTPS support & data deletion inquiry form
- `styles.css` — Responsive editorial tabletop theme stylesheet
- `assets/` — App icon, feature banner, and screenshots
