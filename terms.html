3. Virtual Play Tickets & Reward System

  • Starting an online match consumes 1 virtual play ticket.
  • Tickets automatically refill up to 10 tickets every hour (hourly refills never exceed 10 tickets).
  • Players may earn +2 bonus tickets by watching an optional Google AdMob rewarded advertisement.
  • Offline Play vs AI and Local Pass & Play are completely free and never consume tickets.
  • Virtual tickets, XP points, and ELO ratings have no real-world monetary value, cannot be redeemed for cash, and are non-transferable.

4. Gameplay Rules & Match Conduct

To maintain fair and enjoyable gameplay across global matches:

  • Turn Timers: Players have 15 seconds to complete their move during an active online match. Missing 2 consecutive turns results in an automatic forfeiture by timeout.
  • Disconnections: Intentionally disconnecting or closing the application during an active ranked match awards the victory to the remaining opponent.
  • Multi-Grid Rules: Matches follow standard line-completion rules: 3 in a row on 3×3, 4 in a row on 4×4 and 5×5, and 5 in a row on 6×6 grids.
